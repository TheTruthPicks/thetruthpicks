How the NBA Test Works

  • No Peeking at the Future. The projection model for each season was trained only on seasons before it, then went through that season game by game.
  • Real Prices. Every price is a real US bookmaker line recorded 1 hour before tip-off. We take the best price on offer, the same way the live bot does.
  • Same Rules as Today. The bet only counts if the price beats the fair line of the other bookmakers by 2.5% or more (4% for assists and PRA) and the model agrees. Steals, Pts+Ast, rebound overs and PRA overs are skipped (the backtest showed bookies price those well). Stakes are sized to the edge, with the same adjustments the live bot makes.
  • Max 8 Picks a Day. When more than 8 picks qualify, only the 8 with the most value count, exactly as on the live card. Fewer, stronger picks.
  • Value Parlays. 2 legs from different games, each one of the night's strongest picks (4%+ value), priced at one bookie that has both legs. Stakes are 0.2u–2u, sized to the slip's edge.
  • Graded From the Official Box Score. Win rate is around 50% because most picks are priced around 2.10 (+110). That's where the profit comes from.
  • Playoffs Included. Every season includes the playoffs, the same as the live bot. The playoffs on their own were roughly break-even in testing (253 picks, +1.5u), but the bot still beat the closing line 87% of the time, so the value was there. It's a small sample, and we track it live.
  • 2023-24 Is the Blind Test. Today's rules were built on 2024-25 and 2025-26. We downloaded 2023-24 afterwards and ran it untouched, with a model that had only one earlier season to learn from. It still made a profit and beat the closing line 85% of the time. The two later seasons helped shape the rules, so they're slightly flattering. Expect live results closer to 2023-24.

How the NFL Test Works

  • Real Prices. Every price is a real US bookmaker player-prop line from before kick-off. We take the best price on offer.
  • The Rule. Unders on yardage and volume props (receptions, receiving and rushing yards, rush attempts, completions, tackles and more) where one bookie's price beats the other bookies' fair line by 2%+, one pick per player, and at most 4 picks per game day (the ones with the most value). Overs, touchdown markets and interceptions lost money in testing, so they're never picked.
  • No Peeking at the Future. The projection model for each season was trained only on seasons before it.
  • QB Changes. Every pick is a flat 1 unit, except when a team starts a different quarterback from its last game: picks on that team get 1.5 units. That held up in all three seasons.
  • Graded From the Official Stats.
  • 2023-24 Is the Tougher Test. The core rules were built on later seasons, and the bot never saw 2023 until this test. It still made a profit. The max-4 daily cap was chosen using all three seasons.

What a Backtest Can't Tell You

  • Bookmakers limit winning accounts and lines move fast. Not every price here would have been available to everyone at the same size.
  • Past results, simulated or real, don't guarantee future ones. Expect losing weeks and losing months.
  • Football (soccer) isn't shown yet. It gets added when its bot is finished and tested the same way.
