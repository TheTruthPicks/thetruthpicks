How the NBA Test Works

  • No Peeking at the Future. The projection model for each season was trained only on seasons before it, then went through that season game by game.
  • Real Prices at Licensed Bookies Only. Every price is a real line from a state-licensed US bookmaker (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars and others), recorded 1 hour before tip-off. Offshore bookies are never used, the same as the live bot.
  • A Fair Line Without the Soft Books. The fair price comes from the other bookmakers, leaving out the ones that are slowest to move their lines, so the bot only bets when it beats a sharp price.
  • Same Rules as Today. The bet only counts if the price beats the fair line of the other bookmakers by 2.5% or more (4% for assists and PRA) and the model agrees. Steals, Pts+Ast, rebound overs and PRA overs are skipped (the backtest showed bookies price those well). Picks that have value at two or more licensed bookies get priority. Stakes are sized to the edge and rounded to 0.05u, with the same adjustments the live bot makes.
  • Max 8 Picks a Day. When more than 8 picks qualify, only the 8 with the most value count, exactly as on the live card. Fewer, stronger picks.
  • Value Parlays. 2 legs from different games, each one of the night's strongest picks (4%+ value), priced at one licensed bookie that has both legs. Half-size stakes (0.1u–1u) while we keep testing them.
  • Graded From the Official Box Score. Win rate is around 50% because most picks are priced around 2.10 (+110). That's where the profit comes from.
  • Playoffs Included. Every season includes the playoffs, the same as the live bot. The playoffs on their own were mixed (230 picks, +17.7u, but one losing year) and the bot beat the closing line 76% of the time. It's a small sample, and we track it live.
  • 2023-24 Is the Blind Test. Today's rules were built on 2024-25 and 2025-26. We downloaded 2023-24 afterwards and ran it untouched, with a model that had only one earlier season to learn from. It still made a profit and beat the closing line 79% of the time. The two later seasons helped shape the rules, so they're slightly flattering. Expect live results closer to 2023-24.

How the NFL Test Works

  • Real Prices at Licensed Bookies Only. Every price is a real player-prop line from a state-licensed US bookmaker, from before kick-off. Offshore bookies are never used.
  • The Rule. Unders on yardage and volume props (receptions, receiving and rushing yards, rush attempts, completions, tackles and more) where one bookie's price beats the other bookies' fair line by 2%+, one pick per player, and at most 4 picks per game day (the ones with the most value). Overs, touchdown markets and interceptions lost money in testing, so they're never picked.
  • No Peeking at the Future. The projection model for each season was trained only on seasons before it.
  • QB Changes. Every pick is a flat 1 unit, except when a team starts a different quarterback from its last game: picks on that team get 1.5 units. Picks on teams that are 7+ point underdogs, or in 15+ mph wind at outdoor stadiums, also get 1.5 units. Both held up in all three seasons.
  • Graded From the Official Stats.
  • 2023-24 Is the Tougher Test. The core rules were built on later seasons, and the bot never saw 2023 until this test. It still made a profit. The max-4 daily cap was chosen using all three seasons.

What a Backtest Can't Tell You

  • Bookmakers limit winning accounts and lines move fast. Not every price here would have been available to everyone at the same size.
  • Past results, simulated or real, don't guarantee future ones. Expect losing weeks and losing months.
  • Each pick uses the best price across licensed US bookies. If you bet at one bookie, or at UK bookies, some prices will be lower and so will the profit. Always check the "Don't Bet Below" price on the live card.
  • Some 2025-26 NBA prices for combo and blocks markets are missing from our historical data, so that season has fewer picks than it would have had.
  • Football (soccer) isn't shown yet. It gets added when its bot is finished and tested the same way.
