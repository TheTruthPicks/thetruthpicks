How the Picks Are Made

A data model prices NBA and NFL player props across 20+ bookmakers, strips out the bookmaker margin to find the fair odds, and only flags a bet when a price beats the fair line and the projections agree. It factors in form, minutes, matchups, injuries, pace and schedule.

NBA stakes scale with the size of the edge (0.2u to 2.5u); NFL picks are a flat 1u, or 1.5u when a team changes quarterback, is a 7+ point underdog or plays in 15+ mph wind. Value parlays are 0.5u flat. Losing nights stay on the record with the winning ones. Judge it over hundreds of picks, not a few nights.
